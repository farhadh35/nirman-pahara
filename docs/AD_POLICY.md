# Advertising policy

The app is free and ad-supported, with no paywall. The whole value of the app is
that it tells someone whether the cement, the rod, the brick and the price they
are being given are sound. Ads from the companies selling those things would
destroy that, so the funding model is constrained as follows.

## Category blocklist

No advertising — network-served or direct — from:

- cement, steel/rod, brick, sand, aggregate, tile, paint or construction-chemical
  manufacturers or dealers
- contractors, builders, developers or real-estate marketplaces
- any advertiser whose product the app evaluates

AdMob content-category blocking is configured for these and **re-checked every
quarter**, because network category definitions drift.

## Where ads may appear

Allowed: the home screen, the guide module list, the calculator result screen.

Never: any checklist screen, any finding or verdict screen, the report preview,
or any rights, RTI or complaint screen. No interstitial during an inspection run
— a person standing at a site in the sun does not get an ad between checklist
items.

## Disclosure

Settings carries a **কে এই অ্যাপ চালায়, টাকা কোথা থেকে আসে** page stating plainly
that the app is ad-funded and takes no money from material brands or contractors.
It is not buried.

## Data

Photos, location and inspection records stay on the device. The ad SDK is the
only thing that transmits anything, and the Play data-safety declaration must say
exactly that.
