Nirman Pahara — a Bangla-first Android app that lets a citizen check whether a construction work is being done properly. Public works, or your own house.

The road through your area sank in one monsoon, and on paper the work is finished. You do not need to be an engineer to understand why — you need to know which few things to look at, and when.

Everything works offline. Nothing you record leaves your phone. There is no paywall.

What it does

  • Learn — materials, concrete, steel and your rights, in plain language, with labelled diagrams.
  • Inspect — stage-by-stage checklists for roads, buildings, food godowns, repairs and electrical work, ending in a PDF report with each photograph beside its finding.
  • Calculate — how much material a job needs, and what the contract implies per metre.
  • Check a rate schedule — import the department's schedule and see what is worth asking about.
  • Your rights — where to complain, in what order, with the letters ready to send.

How it talks about what it finds

Every finding is phrased as the standard requires X; what was seen is Y — never as an accusation. That is what an inspecting officer can verify, and it is what keeps the person filing it out of an argument they cannot win.
