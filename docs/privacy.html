Privacy policy — নির্মাণ পাহারা / Nirman Pahara

Last updated 2 September 2026 · bd.nirmanpahara.nirman_pahara
The short version. Nirman Pahara keeps what you record on your own phone. Your inspections, notes, photographs and any location stamped onto them stay on the device and are never uploaded to us. There is no account system, so we do not know who you are. The only thing that leaves the device is whatever the advertising SDK sends, and only when an advertisement is shown.

What the app stores, and where

WhatWhere it is keptLeaves the device?
Inspection answers and notesOn the deviceNo
Photographs taken in the appThe app's private directory, not the galleryOnly if you share them
Location stamped on a photographWith that photograph, on the deviceOnly if you share it
Schedule rates you enterOn the deviceNo
Language, text size, trackOn the deviceNo

Uninstalling the app removes all of it. There is no copy anywhere else.

Permissions, and why each is asked for

Camera

To photograph what you are inspecting. Photographs are written to the app's private directory rather than the shared gallery, so an inspection of a neighbour's contract does not appear in your camera roll.

Location

Stamped onto a photograph so a report can say where a defect was seen. It is requested at the moment of capture and never in the background. If you refuse it, or the phone cannot get a fix, the photograph is still taken and the report says plainly that the location was not recorded — and why.

File access

Only to open a rate schedule you choose yourself from the system file picker. The app cannot browse your files.

Advertising

The app is free and supported by advertising. The advertising SDK may collect device and advertising identifiers in order to serve and measure ads, as described by that network's own policy.

Two limits we place on ourselves:

  • No advertising is accepted from cement, steel, brick, tile, paint, construction-chemical, contracting or real-estate companies — because the app exists to judge those things.
  • No advertising appears on any inspection, finding, report or complaint screen.

Children

The app is intended for adults monitoring construction work. It is not directed at children and knowingly collects nothing from them.

Sharing

Nothing is shared with us. When you tap share, your phone hands the report and its photographs to the app you choose, and what happens next is governed by that app.

Your control

Delete any photograph, or a whole inspection, from inside the app. Uninstalling removes everything. Because nothing is held on a server, there is no data of yours for us to delete on request — we do not have any.
