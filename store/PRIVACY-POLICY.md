## The short version

Nirman Pahara keeps what you record on your own phone. Your inspections, notes,
photographs and any location stamped onto them are stored on the device and are
never uploaded to us. We do not have an account system, so we do not know who
you are.

The one thing that leaves the device is whatever the advertising SDK sends, and
only when an advertisement is shown.

## What the app stores, and where

| What | Where it is kept | Leaves the device? |
|---|---|---|
| Inspection answers and notes | On the device | No |
| Photographs taken in the app | The app's own private directory, not the gallery | Only if you share them yourself |
| Location stamped on a photograph | With that photograph, on the device | Only if you share it yourself |
| Schedule rates you enter | On the device | No |
| Language, text size, track | On the device | No |

Uninstalling the app removes all of it. There is no copy anywhere else.

## Permissions, and why each is asked for

**Camera** — to photograph what you are inspecting. Photographs are written to
the app's private directory rather than the shared gallery, so an inspection of
a neighbour's contract does not appear in your camera roll.

**Location** — stamped onto a photograph so a report can say where a defect was
seen. It is requested at the moment of capture and never in the background. If
you refuse it, or the phone cannot get a fix, the photograph is still taken and
the report says plainly that the location was not recorded.

**Storage / file access** — only to open a rate schedule you choose yourself
from a file picker. The app cannot browse your files.

## Advertising

The app is free and supported by advertising. The advertising SDK may collect
device and advertising identifiers in order to serve and measure ads, as
described by the ad network's own policy.

Two limits we place on ourselves:

- No advertising is accepted from cement, steel, brick, tile, paint,
  construction-chemical, contracting or real-estate companies, because the app
  exists to judge those things.
- No advertising appears on any inspection, finding, report or complaint
  screen.

## Children

The app is intended for adults monitoring construction work. It is not directed
at children and collects nothing knowingly from them.

## Sharing

Nothing is shared with us. When you tap share, your phone hands the report and
its photographs to the app you choose, and what happens next is governed by that
app.

## Your control

Delete any photograph, or a whole inspection, from inside the app. Uninstalling
removes everything.
